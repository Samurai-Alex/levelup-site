Habit tracker for Windows

LEVEL UP is a desktop app that turns habits into a game: every tick earns XP, your level grows, titles and rewards unlock. Free during the beta.

What it does

  • Pick ready-made habits or add your own, with an icon and colour
  • XP for every tick, levels, 30+ titles, badges and rewards
  • Day streaks and a streak freeze — 2 days a month if you fall ill
  • Reminders at the right time
  • Heat maps and weekly, monthly and yearly reviews
  • Planner: today’s tasks and the main goal of the week

Why on your computer

Your phone distracts you with notifications, and you spend the day at your computer anyway. Habits, money and fitness in one window, with no account and no ads. Your data stays on your PC; no internet needed.

More than habits

The same app has expense tracking and budgets, fitness and health, English practice and a trading journal. Hide the tabs you don’t need.
