Quit bad habits

A ready set in LEVEL UP: no smoking, no alcohol or energy drinks, social media under 30 minutes and no impulse buys. Every day “without” is a tick and XP.

How to add it

Download LEVEL UP and open it: the welcome window has a “Ready sets” tab. Click “Quit bad habits” — its habits get ticked, and you can untick any or add your own. Later you can add the set from the “Add habits” window — adding it again never creates duplicates.

Why it works

Quitting is hard because you don’t see a reward — you just give something up. Here every clean day earns XP, and the streak grows in front of you, so you don’t want to break it. Progress shows by day, week and month.
