In Notion you build a habit tracker from a template: a table, checkboxes, formulas. LEVEL UP is a ready-made Windows app where everything is already set up, and every tick earns XP.

When Notion is better

If you already keep notes and projects in Notion, need access from your phone or to share with a team, and enjoy shaping tables to fit you.

When LEVEL UP is better

If Notion templates keep getting abandoned: a table doesn’t remind you or reward you. Here a habit takes one click, XP and levels grow before your eyes, and expense tracking and fitness sit right next to it. No internet or account needed.

Start in a minute

Nothing to build: pick habits from a ready-made list or a set for your goal — say, productivity habits or an emergency fund.

Questions

Can I use both?

Of course: notes and projects in Notion, daily habits and spending in LEVEL UP.

Does it sync between devices?

No, LEVEL UP keeps your data on one computer. You can put the automatic backups in a OneDrive or other cloud folder.

Is it free?

Yes, during the beta. Everyone who downloads the app during the beta gets the full version at 50% off.
