How to add it

Download LEVEL UP and open it: the welcome window has a “Ready sets” tab. Click “Healthy sleep” — its habits get ticked, and you can untick any or add your own. Later you can add the set from the “Add habits” window — adding it again never creates duplicates.

Why it works

Sleep isn’t ruined by one late night but by small things every day: evening coffee, a screen in bed, an energy drink instead of rest. The Health section shows how much you sleep, and the app points out links — for example, how coffee affects your sleep.
