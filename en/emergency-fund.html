How to add it

Download LEVEL UP and open it: the welcome window has a “Ready sets” tab. Click “Emergency fund” — its habits get ticked, and you can untick any or add your own. Later you can add the set from the “Add habits” window — adding it again never creates duplicates.

Why it works

An emergency fund is usually 3–6 months of expenses. To build it, you need to know where money goes, avoid emotional spending and save regularly. A savings goal shows how much is left, and expense tracking shows where the money leaks.
