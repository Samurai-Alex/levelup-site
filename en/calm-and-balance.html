How to add it

Download LEVEL UP and open it: the welcome window has a “Ready sets” tab. Click “Calm and balance” — its habits get ticked, and you can untick any or add your own. Later you can add the set from the “Add habits” window — adding it again never creates duplicates.

Why it works

Stress builds up quietly, and it’s best let go of a little every day too. Ten minutes of meditation, a few lines of notes and a walk help you breathe out. The Health section shows how your mood changes.
