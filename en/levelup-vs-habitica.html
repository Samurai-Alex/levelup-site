LEVEL UP vs Habitica

Both apps turn habits into a game: ticks earn XP and levels. Habitica is an online RPG with an avatar, quests and a party. LEVEL UP is a Windows app that puts your budget, fitness and health next to your habits, and keeps your data on your computer.

When Habitica is better

If you want to tick habits on your phone, play with friends in a party and go on quests together, that’s exactly what Habitica is built for. It has been around for years, with a big community and open source code.

When LEVEL UP is better

If you spend the day at your computer and want habits, money and fitness in one window. No account, no ads, your data never leaves your PC, and the game doesn’t punish you: a missed day costs no health, and you can freeze a streak for 2 days a month. More on the habit tracker for Windows.

Start in a minute

Pick habits from a ready-made list or a whole set for your goal — say, a morning routine or habits for students. Untick anything you don’t need.

Questions

Can I import my Habitica data?

Not yet, there’s no import. But habits are quick to add from the ready-made list or a set.

Is LEVEL UP on phones?

Not yet — Windows 10 and 11 only.

Is it free?

Yes, during the beta. Everyone who downloads the app during the beta gets the full version at 50% off.
