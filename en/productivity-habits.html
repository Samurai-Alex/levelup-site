How to add it

Download LEVEL UP and open it: the welcome window has a “Ready sets” tab. Click “Productivity” — its habits get ticked, and you can untick any or add your own. Later you can add the set from the “Add habits” window — adding it again never creates duplicates.

Why it works

Productivity isn’t working more — it’s not scattering your attention. One hour without notifications gets more done than half a day with them. The focus timer helps you hold that hour, and the daily plan tells you what to spend it on.
